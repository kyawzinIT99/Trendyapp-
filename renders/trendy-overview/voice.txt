Trendy is a shopping app designed for Myanmar.
Customers browse in English or Burmese. Prices are in kyats, and delivery stays inside the country.
The catalog lives on the phone, with a price and a rating on every item.
When someone checks out, n8n sends that order to one private Google Sheet. Customers never see the sheet.
The owner calls the courier, then updates the status on the sheet.
Tracking follows that sheet. Each phone shows only its own order: placed, confirmed, pickup, processing, and delivered home.
One shop. One sheet. Built for Myanmar.
